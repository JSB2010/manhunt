Manhunt Crews

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Manhunt Crews is a game for friends: two or three crews, one to five phones per crew, and scheduled location pings. This policy explains what the app records, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to delete it. Questions: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • Account. Your email address, the name you choose and your @handle. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Location during rounds. Only on the phones playing a round, and only during a round you explicitly start (plus a short ready check), including while the screen is locked. Tracking stops when the round ends. We use precise location to release scheduled pings to the seeking crews (each ping comes from the freshest fix of any hiding-crew phone) and to compute each crew's distance and route after the round.
  • Catch photos. Photos the seeking crew takes in the app to claim a catch. Location and camera metadata are removed on our server. Photos may show bystanders.
  • Game and social data. Nights, rounds, scores, rosters, friends, groups, and the names you save for crewmates who don't use the app.
  • Phone status during rounds. Location permission, tracking, battery and connection state, so the crews can see whether pings will be accurate. Other phones see only a coarse summary per crew, such as how many of its phones are live.
  • Push notifications and Live Activities. A device token if you allow notifications. While you play a round, a token that keeps the round on your Lock Screen up to date; it's deleted within 12 hours. Neither notifications nor the Lock Screen ever show a location.
  • Server logs. Request metadata used to keep the service running. Coordinates are removed from logs.

We don't show ads, we don't sell or share data for advertising, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and the app contains no third-party analytics.

Who can see what

  • While a round is running, the seeking crews receive only the scheduled pings, never the hiders' live route or latest position. Each phone sees only its own trail. Organizing a night gives no extra access.
  • After a round, the phones that played it can see each crew's route, the pings and the distances. Routes are shown only if every phone in the round agreed to post-round route sharing. A catch photo is visible to the hiding crew and the crew that claimed the catch (in a Together round, every seeking crew). A phone that joins a night later can't open the routes, pings or photos of rounds it didn't play.
  • Friends see your name and handle. Being friends never shares your location.
  • Service providers. Data is stored and processed by Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) in the United States, and push notifications are delivered by Apple. They process data only to run the service.
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Raw GPS points: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Simplified routes, pings and stats: kept until the night is deleted or you delete your data from it.
  • Catch photos: deleted after 7 days if not confirmed, and after 30 days if confirmed, unless every crew in the night chooses to keep them.
  • Account and social data: kept until you delete your account.

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete my data (per night, in History) removes your tracks, the photos you took and the pings that came from your phone from that night.
  • Delete night (organizer) removes the whole night for everyone.
  • Delete account (Settings) removes your profile, handle, devices, social graph and data from every night. In nights shared with other phones, your part is removed or anonymized.
  • You can stop location access at any time in iOS Settings; rounds then show missing fixes as missing.
  • You can also ask us to access or delete your data at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Children

Manhunt Crews is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
