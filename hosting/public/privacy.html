Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Manhunt Crews is a game for friends: two crews, one phone each, and scheduled location pings. This policy explains what the app records, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to delete it. Questions: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • Account. Your email address, the name you choose and your @handle. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Location during rounds. Only on a crew's phone, and only during a round you explicitly start (plus a short ready check), including while the screen is locked. Tracking stops when the round ends. We use precise location to release scheduled pings to the other crew and to compute distances and routes after the round.
  • Catch photos. Photos the seeking crew takes in the app to claim a catch. Location and camera metadata are removed on our server. Photos may show bystanders.
  • Game and social data. Nights, rounds, scores, rosters, friends, groups, and the names you save for crewmates who don't use the app.
  • Phone status during rounds. Location permission, tracking, battery and connection state, so both operators can see whether pings will be accurate. The other crew sees only a coarse summary.
  • Push notifications. A device token if you allow notifications. Notifications never contain locations.
  • Server logs. Request metadata used to keep the service running. Coordinates are removed from logs.

We don't show ads, we don't sell or share data for advertising, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and the app contains no third-party analytics.

Who can see what

  • While a round is running, the other crew receives only the scheduled pings, never your live route or latest position. Hosting a night gives no extra access.
  • After a round, the two operators of that round can see both routes, pings, distances and the catch photo. Routes are shown only if both operators agreed to post-round route sharing.
  • Friends see your name and handle. Being friends never shares your location.
  • Service providers. Data is stored and processed by Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) in the United States, and push notifications are delivered by Apple. They process data only to run the service.
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Raw GPS points: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Simplified routes, pings and stats: kept until the night is deleted or you delete your data from it.
  • Catch photos: deleted after 7 days if not confirmed, and after 30 days if confirmed, unless both operators choose to keep them.
  • Account and social data: kept until you delete your account.

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete my data (per night, in History) removes your tracks and the photos you took from that night.
  • Delete night (organizer) removes the whole night for everyone.
  • Delete account (Settings) removes your profile, handle, devices, social graph and data from every night. In nights shared with another operator, your part is removed or anonymized.
  • You can stop location access at any time in iOS Settings; rounds then show missing fixes as missing.
  • You can also ask us to access or delete your data at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Children

Manhunt Crews is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
