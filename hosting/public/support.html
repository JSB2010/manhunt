Common questions

Pings are late or missing. On the hiding crew's phones, set Location to "While Using the App" with Precise Location on, keep Low Power Mode off, and don't force-quit the app during a round. The Phone Status sheet in a round shows what's wrong. If no hider fix has arrived at a ping's time, the ping waits and goes out, marked late, as soon as one does. Positions are never invented.

Notifications are late. Game timing never depends on notifications; the clock comes from the server. Open the app to see the current phase.

I can't join a night. Invite codes expire after an hour and work once. Ask the organizer for a new one.

How do I delete my data? Per night: History → the night → Delete My Data. Everything: Settings → Delete Account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Safety. Any phone in a round can pause it for safety at any time. Play only where it's legal and safe, and never chase by car in a way that breaks traffic laws.
